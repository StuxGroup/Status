Privacy Policy

What the Stux.Group status page collects: nothing about you.

Effective: 29 September 2026

The short version

status.stux.group is a static page that shows whether Stux.Group's services are up. It has no accounts, forms, analytics, tracking or advertising, and it collects no personal data about you.

Hosting

The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its domain is managed by Cloudflare, which may also proxy requests to it. They may process your IP address and browser details to deliver the page and protect it from abuse, under GitHub's privacy statement and Cloudflare's privacy policy. We don't receive or keep those logs.

Live status data

To stay current, the page reloads its status data from this site and from raw.githubusercontent.com (GitHub). Only Stux.Group's own monitoring data is fetched; nothing about you is sent.

Images

Stux.Group logos are loaded from global.media.stux.group, without cookies and without tracking.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you can ask what personal data we hold about you. For this page the answer is none, but you're welcome to ask at legal@stux.group.
